The card What's translated

Story Mode1,968 scenes

All 35 chapters of dialogue and narration. Speaker names, page breaks and text boxes are laid out to fit the game's three-line window, with no lines cut off or running past the box.

Main menu, Exhibition, Practice, Replay, Data Book, Options and save/load messages, including the scrolling help line at the bottom of each screen.

In the corner Translation notes

Names follow the official English

Characters, techniques and places use the spellings fans know from the English manga and anime: Ippo Makunouchi, Mamoru Takamura, Volg Zangief, Jimmy Sisphar, the Dempsey Roll.

Dialects read as personality

Sendo's Osaka swagger, Take's Kyushu warmth and Volg's careful, polite Japanese come through in word choice and rhythm, not phonetic spelling.

"Wh-what?! Makunouchi's withdrawing?! That's the dumbest thing I ever heard!" (Sendo)

Boxing terms are real boxing terms

Infighter, outboxer, boxer-puncher, knockdowns, the final bell, OPBF. Weight classes and "Rookie King" match the series' English usage.

Fitted to the screen

Long names were shortened only where a box is narrow, such as the venue cards ("Osaka Pref. Gym", "Korakuen Final") and trophy names.

Under the hood For ROM hackers

  • Story scenes are rebuilt with a larger text capacity (a small code patch), so English never overflows the game's scene buffer.
  • Executable strings that outgrew their slots are moved to a new area of memory and every pointer to them is updated. Nothing else in the program moves.
  • Menu widgets that show values at runtime get more room, padded invisibly, so names and help lines display in full.
  • Graphics are edited in the game's own texture format with the original palette, so every pixel outside the redrawn text is unchanged.
  • The game's internal file table is updated for every changed file, and each build is checked file by file against the original.

Patching About two minutes

  1. Download Delta Patcher and the .xdelta file from the release.
  2. Make a copy of your Japanese ISO. Delta Patcher changes the file you pick, so patch the copy and keep the original safe.
  3. Open Delta Patcher. Next to Original file, click the folder icon and choose the copy. Next to XDelta patch, choose the .xdelta file.
  4. Click Apply patch and wait a few seconds for the success message. The copy is now the English game; rename it if you like.
  5. Play it in PPSSPP, or on a PSP with custom firmware. If Delta Patcher reports an error, your ISO is a different dump: its SHA-1 must match the one below.

Known limits Small print

  • 41 lesser-known roster boxers have no public record of how their names are read. They use the most likely reading; corrections are welcome.
  • The tiny sponsor print under the arena banners, the title logo, one promotional picture and the kanji drawn into the Game Over artwork stay in Japanese.
  • Wireless (ad hoc) play hasn't been tested.

Found something? Reports

Open an issue on the GitHub page with a screenshot and say where you were: the mode, story chapter or menu screen. Bad lines, cut-off text, leftover Japanese and wrong names all get fixed in the next update. A checklist for testers comes with the patch (TESTING.txt).
